Ridgeline Hotels Team Member Resources

Scheduling and Shift Swaps

Effective this year. Owner: Hotel Operations.

When schedules are posted

Schedules are posted every Thursday for the two weeks starting the following Monday.

Swapping a shift

You can swap a shift with a team member in the same role at the same hotel. Both of you must agree in the scheduling system at least 48 hours before the shift, and your supervisor must approve the swap. A swap can't put either person over 40 hours in a week.

Holiday pay

Team members who work on New Year's Day, Thanksgiving, or Christmas Day are paid 1.5 times their regular rate for that shift.

Calling out

If you can't work a scheduled shift, call your supervisor at least 2 hours before it starts. Missing a shift without calling is recorded as a no-call, no-show.

Ridgeline Hotels is a fictional company created for GBUS 562.
