Ridgeline Hotels Team Member Resources

Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Effective this year. Owner: People and Culture.

How you earn PTO

Full-time team members earn 12 PTO days a year in their first three years and 18 PTO days a year after that, credited monthly. Part-time team members earn PTO in proportion to their scheduled hours. Seasonal team members don't earn PTO.

Carrying PTO into next year

You can carry up to 3 unused PTO days into the next calendar year. Any unused days above 3 are paid out at your regular rate in the first paycheck of January.

Requesting PTO

Request PTO through your hotel's scheduling system. Requests of 4 days or fewer need your supervisor's approval at least 14 days ahead. Requests of 5 or more days in a row need your supervisor's approval at least 30 days ahead. Requests that include a blackout date need your general manager's approval.

Blackout dates

Each hotel sets up to 10 blackout dates a year, around its busiest events, and posts them in January. PTO isn't normally approved on a blackout date.

Borrowing PTO you haven't earned yet

If a planned absence is longer than your balance, you can borrow up to 3 PTO days from what you will earn in the next six months. Borrowed days need your general manager's approval, and the request must be made at least 30 days ahead. If you leave Ridgeline before you earn the borrowed days back, their value is deducted from your final paycheck.

Sick days

Team members receive 6 paid sick days a year, separate from PTO. Sick days don't carry over and aren't paid out. Call your supervisor at least 2 hours before your shift starts.

Ridgeline Hotels is a fictional company created for GBUS 562.
