Ridgeline Hotels Team Member Resources

Reporting Concerns and Workplace Safety

Effective this year. Owner: People and Culture.

Harassment by guests or coworkers

Ridgeline doesn't tolerate harassment of team members by anyone, including guests. If a guest or coworker harasses you, you can report it to your supervisor, to any manager, or directly to People and Culture. A member of People and Culture will contact you within 1 business day of a report.

If your supervisor doesn't act

If you reported a concern to your supervisor and nothing was done, contact People and Culture directly. You can also call the confidential Team Member Line at any time.

No retaliation

Ridgeline prohibits retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith. Report any retaliation to People and Culture right away.

Workplace injuries

Report any injury at work to your supervisor before the end of your shift, even if it seems minor. Your supervisor will complete an incident report with you.

Ridgeline Hotels is a fictional company created for GBUS 562.
