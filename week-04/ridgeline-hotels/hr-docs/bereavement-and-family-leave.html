Ridgeline Hotels Team Member Resources

Bereavement and Family Leave

Effective this year. Owner: People and Culture.

Bereavement leave

After the death of a spouse or partner, child, parent, or sibling, you receive up to 5 paid days off. After the death of a grandparent, grandchild, or in-law, you receive up to 3 paid days off. Days don't need to be taken in a row, but must be used within 60 days.

How to request bereavement leave

Tell your supervisor as soon as you can. No documentation is needed. Bereavement leave doesn't reduce your PTO or sick balance.

Family and medical leave

Team members with at least 12 months of service can take up to 12 weeks of unpaid, job-protected leave for the birth or adoption of a child or a serious health condition, under federal law. Contact People and Culture at least 30 days ahead when you can.

Parental leave

Full-time team members with at least 12 months of service receive 2 weeks of paid parental leave after the birth or adoption of a child, within the first six months.
