ANSWER
No. The outgoing international wire transfer fee is $45 [4: consumer-fee-schedule.html]. A member service representative may approve a refund without anyone else's approval only for a single fee of $30 or less [2: fee-refund-guide.html]. Because $45 is more than $30, the refund needs the branch manager's approval [1: fee-refund-guide.html].

Other points that may apply:
- If the member has already had a refund in the last 90 days, or is asking for several fees to be refunded at once, branch manager approval is also needed [1: fee-refund-guide.html].
- If the refund would bring the member's total refunds above $150 in a 90-day period, or if it is for a business account, the Member Services Director must approve it [3: fee-refund-guide.html].
- Whoever approves it, record the fee, the amount refunded, the reason, and the approver's name in the member's account notes. Unrecorded refunds may be reversed during the monthly audit [5: fee-refund-guide.html].
