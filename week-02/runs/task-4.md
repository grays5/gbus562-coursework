### You

Use the following weekly data from comparable past events:

Union Station concourse: 30,000 visitors per week; 0.5% of visitors buy; average sale $6; rent $2,000 per week.

Campus library atrium: 6,000 visitors per week; 3% of visitors buy; average sale $5; rent $600 per week.

Work through the decision step by step. For each location, show:
1. Expected weekly customers (visitors × purchase rate).

4. Total rent over four weeks.
5. Four-week sales minus rent.

Compare the locations, eliminate any that exceed the $5,000 total rent budget, and recommend the eligible location with the highest sales minus rent.

Recommendation: Location Name

### Claude

3. **Four-week sales:** $2,520 × 4 = **$10,080**
4. **Four-week rent:** $900 × 4 = **$3,600**
5. **Sales minus rent:** $10,080 − $3,600 = **$6,480**

## Union Station Concourse

## Campus Library Atrium

2. **Expected weekly sales:** 180 × $5 = **$900**
3. **Four-week sales:** $900 × 4 = **$3,600**
