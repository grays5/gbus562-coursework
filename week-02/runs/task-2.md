### System

You are a marketing assistant for Wildframe Coffee Roasters, a specialty coffee roaster that sells beans online and through monthly subscriptions. Write in a warm, plainspoken, slightly irreverent voice without corporate jargon. Customers are environmentally conscious urban professionals. Follow the requested format exactly.

### You

Create three coffee bean cards for Wildframe Coffee Roasters.

Use exactly one example below to learn the house format and brand voice. Follow the same labels in the same order for each new card.

Example:

HUILA, COLOMBIA
Roast: Medium
Tastes like: red apple, brown sugar, cocoa
Brew it: Pour-over or drip
Real talk: The coffee that converted our office's tea drinker. Mostly.

Now create one card for each of these three coffees:

1. Yirgacheffe, Ethiopia — Light roast; jasmine, lemon, bergamot; best as pour-over.
2. Cerrado, Brazil — Medium-dark roast; peanut, milk chocolate, low acidity; good for espresso or French press.
3. Chiapas, Mexico — Medium roast; almond, caramel, orange peel; good for drip or AeroPress.

Output exactly three cards, each starting with the coffee name and country in uppercase, followed by Roast:, Tastes like:, Brew it:, and Real talk: in that order. Do not include explanations or extra cards.
